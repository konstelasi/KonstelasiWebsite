## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Konstelasi Chat**. This service is managed and operated by PT Konstelasi Teknologi Internasional ("we", "us", "the Company"), based in Yogyakarta, Indonesia.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use and safeguard your information when you use our AI chatbot and customer support through the third-party platforms WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook Messenger.

## 2. Information we collect

To provide automated AI responses and live support, we collect the following information when you interact with our app.

- **Public profile information.** First name, last name, profile picture, and the user ID specific to each platform (PSID for Messenger, IGSID for Instagram, WAID for WhatsApp).
- **Communication content.** The text messages, images, videos or files you send us in the chat.
- **Technical metadata.** When each message was sent, its delivery status, and your language preference.

## 3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect for these purposes.

- **AI chatbot service.** Processing incoming messages with artificial intelligence to give instant, relevant answers that fit the context.
- **Human admin support (human in the loop).** Letting our admin staff see the conversation history so they can step in when the AI can't resolve an issue or when you ask for a person.
- **Customer relationship management.** Recognising you as a unique user so the context of the conversation carries over to future conversations.
- **Legal compliance.** Meeting the legal obligations that apply in the jurisdiction of the Republic of Indonesia.

## 4. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on our secure servers, and access is strictly limited to authorised personnel. We apply industry-standard technical security measures to prevent unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure.

We do not sell, trade or rent users' personal identification information to anyone. We may share generic, aggregated demographic information that isn't linked to any personal identification information with our business partners for statistical analysis.

## 5. Your rights and how to delete your data

In line with the Meta Platform Policy and applicable privacy regulations, you have the right to ask us to delete the personal data we hold about you.

To have your data deleted from our servers, follow these steps.

1. Send an email to [hello@konstelasi.co.id](mailto:hello@konstelasi.co.id).
2. Use the subject line "Data Deletion Request - [Your Name/User ID]".
3. In the body of the email, include these details so we can verify the request.
   - The platform you used (WhatsApp, Instagram or Messenger).
   - Your phone number (for WhatsApp) or username (for Instagram or Messenger).
   - A short statement that you are requesting deletion of your data.

Our team will process your request and permanently remove your data from PT Konstelasi Teknologi Internasional's databases within **3 (three) business days**. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.
